PRACTICAL ACCESSIBILITY SUPPORT

Your team doesn’t need more accessibility theory. They need to know what to do next.

Your developers may know how to build the website without knowing every accessibility requirement. Your designers may understand contrast but have questions about focus states, interaction patterns, or error handling. Your content team may be introducing accessibility issues without realizing it.

That is where consulting and training become useful.

EqualAccess. helps your team understand the accessibility decisions connected to their work, solve implementation questions, and build repeatable practices that make accessibility easier to maintain.

We can support your team whether you’re starting from an audit, preparing a redesign, working through remediation, or trying to improve the way accessibility is handled going forward.

ACCESSIBILITY CONSULTING

Bring accessibility expertise into the work already happening.

You may not need another report. Sometimes your team needs someone who can review a decision, explain a requirement, help prioritize an audit, or work through a difficult accessibility problem.

EqualAccess. provides focused consulting around the areas where accessibility intersects with your website, development process, and team.

  1. 01

    Accessibility Roadmaps & Prioritization

    Turn a long list of accessibility issues into a practical plan.

    We help organize findings by impact, recurrence, user journey, technical dependency, and implementation effort so your team knows where to begin and what should happen next.

    Useful for: existing audits, large issue backlogs, redesigns, remediation projects, and teams that need a clearer path forward.

  2. 02

    Remediation Guidance

    Have your own developers making the fixes?

    We can work through accessibility findings with them, explain why an issue exists, discuss appropriate remediation approaches, and review implementation questions as they arise.

    For teams that would rather hand the remediation work to us, EqualAccess. also provides Code-Level Remediation.

  3. 03

    Design & Development Reviews

    Accessibility decisions happen before a page reaches production.

    EqualAccess. can review designs, components, prototypes, or work in progress to identify potential barriers earlier and provide guidance before those patterns are repeated across the website.

    W3C recommends evaluating accessibility throughout development. Read the W3C guidance.

  4. 04

    Components & Design Systems

    Reusable components can either prevent accessibility problems or repeat them everywhere.

    We help teams evaluate common interface patterns—including navigation, forms, dialogs, tabs, accordions, buttons, alerts, menus, and other interactive components—and document accessibility considerations for future use.

  5. 05

    Accessibility Workflow & QA Guidance

    Make accessibility part of the process instead of a final check.

    We can help your team determine where accessibility reviews belong within design, development, content publishing, QA, releases, and ongoing maintenance.

    The goal is a workflow your team can actually follow—not an oversized governance program nobody uses.

  6. 06

    Third-Party & Vendor Guidance

    Technology outside your codebase still shapes the experience.

    Plugins, apps, embedded services, booking systems, ecommerce tools, and other third-party technology can introduce barriers your team does not directly control.

    We can help identify accessibility concerns, document limitations, evaluate available options, and determine practical next steps.

HOW IT WORKS

Consulting that starts with what your team actually needs.

We start with your project, the people involved, and the priorities that matter. Consulting and training can support any part of our five-step accessibility process.

Training sessions, implementation reviews, technical guidance, and working sessions are scoped around your real work.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Understand your website, existing findings, team responsibilities, and the questions you need help answering.

  2. 02

    Manual Testing

    Investigate real interactions and help your team understand the barriers, user impact, and priorities.

  3. 03

    Code-Level Remediation

    Apply practical guidance to your team’s implementation, or bring us in to make the agreed changes.

  4. 04

    Retest & Verify

    Show how changes should be tested and verify affected experiences within the agreed scope.

  5. 05

    Documentation

    Leave your team with clear records, implementation guidance, and reference material they can keep using.

A consulting or training engagement does not automatically include every stage. We define the support, testing, and deliverables with you before work begins.

STANDARDS & REQUIREMENTS

Help your team understand what accessibility requirements mean for the work they control.

Accessibility requirements can come from different places: internal standards, procurement requirements, WCAG conformance goals, or legal and regulatory obligations.

EqualAccess. can help your technical and content teams understand the accessibility requirements relevant to the scope of their work and translate them into practical design, development, testing, and publishing practices.

WCAGConnect success criteria to your website, components, content, and testing. ADA Website AccessibilityIdentify and address digital accessibility barriers on your website. EqualAccess. provides technical accessibility services and guidance. We do not provide legal advice or determine your organization’s legal obligations.
